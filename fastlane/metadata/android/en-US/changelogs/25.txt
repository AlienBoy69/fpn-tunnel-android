Connecting is resilient now. If your network blocks Mozilla's sign-in host (some ISPs filter the Fastly range it sits on), the app no longer fails every connect: your VPN pass is cached on the device, kept fresh in the background, and a recently expired pass still connects during a short grace window. Sign-in also moved to Mozilla's current OAuth authorization flow, and when Mozilla's anti-bot check does block a sign-in, the app now says so plainly and points you to web sign-in.
