Split tunneling is now fully yours. The built-in bypasses that used to be pinned and locked (Termux, the Muse app, Sina Bank, FPN Tunnel) are ordinary list entries now — check or uncheck any app, including those. Your current setup carries over unchanged: the old defaults simply start out checked, and nothing reroutes until you change it. (One structural exception: the app always keeps itself out of its own tunnel — its engine sockets are unprotected, so its own traffic would loop and the tunnel could never connect.)
