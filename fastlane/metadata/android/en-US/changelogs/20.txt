Connection-state UI fix. The main screen read connection state from the internal engine instead of the tunnel itself, so it could show "Connected" while the VPN was down (or the reverse), and reopening the app while connected restarted the tunnel. The VPN service is now the single source of truth for the connect button, the Quick Settings tile, the Connection Doctor and the fox animation. Verified on a real device; live-tested on TCI Wi-Fi and Irancell LTE. Includes the Doctor fix from v4.2.1.
